# Customer menu redesign

The homepage and QR menu share the category bar, catalogue, bilingual search and product detail dialog. The homepage reads the checked-in catalogue. The QR menu uses the live availability feed where configured and falls back to the same catalogue on static hosting or API failure. Search indexes the catalogue locally in Italian and English.

The six browsing categories group the existing 112 products. Product slugs, descriptions, source categories, base prices and variants retain their existing catalogue values. Crumbl Cookies displays the original published €3.50–€5.00 range when the item has its original €3.50 base price. The coffee add-on prices and the daily lunch notice carry over from the printed menu.

## Photos and 3D

The [media inventory](MENU-MEDIA-INVENTORY.md) identifies two mismatched photos and 37 images with unverified usage rights. Both menus display category artwork for those entries. The two mismatches are Acquainbrick and Windspiel Premium Dry. The manifest and original files remain available for review. The menus label generated artwork as illustrative and identify stock references in product details.

The 13 reviewed models keep their GLB and USDZ assets. The product detail opens in photo mode. Selecting **3D** loads the model, and the photo remains available if loading fails. The AR control follows the browser's model-viewer capability check. Chromium renders were verified for all 13 models. Native Android and iPhone placement still needs a physical device check.
